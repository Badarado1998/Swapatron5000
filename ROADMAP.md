# Roadmap

Five phases. Each one ends with something you can see or touch. Dates will be added once the site is approved.

## Phase 1: Design (we are here)

**Goal:** agree on what we are building.

- [ ] Site approved by the lab and the property owner
- [ ] Standard bin chosen (this sets the module size)
- [ ] Community design sketches collected
- [ ] One shell design picked
- [ ] Cut list and full wishlist written from that design

**How to help:** share a design sketch, vote on sketches, suggest a bin.

## Phase 2: Gather

**Goal:** every item on the wishlist is marked `received`.

**How to help:** pledge parts and supplies. This is where most of the easy wins live.

## Phase 3: Build day

**Goal:** a finished, weatherproof box, built together at the lab.

**How to help:** show up, bring tools, bring kids.

## Phase 4: Open the box

**Goal:** the box is installed, stocked, signed, and has a weekly check rota.

**How to help:** donate your spare parts, join the rota, tell people.

## Phase 5: Make it smart

**Goal:** add power, lighting, sensors, alerts, and inventory, one module at a time.

**How to help:** join the electronics, software, or UI and UX teams. This work can start any time, but the box should work with no electronics at all first.

## After that

Help another town build box number two.
