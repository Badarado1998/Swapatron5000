# Roadmap and timeline

> **Nothing here is decided.** Every date and plan on this page is a proposal. The whole schedule also depends on the site host approving the location. Tell us what you think: see [how to have your say](docs/have-your-say.md).

## The proposed timeline at a glance

| Phase | Proposed dates | What happens | How you can help |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Listen | Mon Nov 23, 2026 to Sun Jan 10, 2027 | We collect ideas, sketches, and concerns | Share a sketch, answer an open question, tell a friend |
| 2. Decide | Mon Jan 11 to Sun Jan 31, 2027 | We summarize what we heard, propose decisions, and take final comments | Read the summary and comment |
| 3. Gather | Mon Feb 1 to Sun Mar 14, 2027 | We collect every item on the wishlist | Pledge or drop off materials |
| 4. Build | Sat Mar 20, 2027 | Build day, indoors at the lab | Come and build |
| 5. Install and open | Sat Apr 10 (install), Sat Apr 24, 2027 (opening) | The box goes in the ground and opens | Stock it, join the upkeep rota |
| 6. Look after it | Every week from Apr 24, 2027 | Recurring upkeep | Take a week on the rota |
| 7. Make it smart | Any time after opening | Power, light, sensors, software | Join a team |

Why this long? The listening phase runs over the winter holidays, and posts can not be set in frozen ground. A spring opening is realistic. A faster one is not.

## Phase 1: Listen (Nov 23, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027)

**Goal:** hear from as many people as possible before anything is chosen.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| Nov 23 to Nov 29 | Set up: create the issues and labels, name interim maintainers, ask the lab for site approval |
| Nov 30 to Dec 6 | Soft launch: invite people at the lab first, so the first sketches and comments are in place. Meet Swapatron drop-in on Thu Dec 3, with nothing built to see yet |
| Mon Nov 30 | The [founding team](docs/founding-team.md) call opens to invited people. It opens to everyone on Dec 7 and closes Sun Jan 10 |
| From Mon Dec 7 | Public launch: share widely, using the [share kit](docs/share-kit.md). Draw Swapatron sketch day on Sat Dec 12 |
| Thu Jan 7 | "This won't work because..." night |
| Sun Jan 10 | Listening closes |

That leaves about five weeks of public listening, with the holidays in the middle. If turnout is thin in early January, the close date should move back, and every later date with it.

- Design sketches are open
- Open questions are posted as issues (see [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md))
- Site approval is requested from the lab and the property owner

**Closes:** Sunday, January 10, 2027. Ideas that arrive later are still welcome, but may miss the first box.

## Phase 2: Decide (Jan 11 to Jan 31, 2027)

**Goal:** turn what we heard into a plan, in the open.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| Jan 11 to Jan 17 | Maintainers post a summary of every open question and a proposed answer for each |
| Jan 18 to Jan 31 | Final comment period. Anyone can object or suggest changes. Decision night on Thu Jan 21 |
| By Jan 31 | Decisions are recorded in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) with the reasons |

## Phase 3: Gather (Feb 1 to Mar 14, 2027)

**Goal:** every item on the wishlist is marked `received`.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| By Sun Feb 7 | Cut list and final wishlist published from the chosen design |
| Feb 8 to Mar 14 | Pledges and drop-offs. Feed Swapatron parts drive on Sat Feb 20 |
| Sun Mar 14 | Materials check. If key items are missing, build day moves |

Supplies that any design will need, such as anti-static bags and desiccant, can be pledged right now.

## Phase 4: Build day (Sat Mar 20, 2027)

**Goal:** a finished box, built together, indoors. See the [build day plan](docs/build-day.md).

## Phase 5: Install and open (April 2027)

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| Week of Apr 5 | Call 811 to have underground lines marked before any digging |
| Sat Apr 10 | Install day. Backup date: Sat Apr 17 |
| Sat Apr 24 | Opening day, with food trucks, a celebration, and a technology petting zoo. The box is stocked and the rota starts |

## Phase 6: Look after it (from Apr 24, 2027)

Weekly, monthly, and seasonal upkeep. See the [maintenance schedule](docs/maintenance.md).

**Sat Jul 24, 2027:** 90 day review. What worked, what broke, what to change.

## Phase 7: Make it smart (after opening)

Power, lighting, sensors, alerts, inventory, and any lock. Planning can start any time, but the box should work with no electronics first.

## Want to change this timeline?

Good. Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) and say which date should move and why.
