# Roadmap and timeline

> **Nothing here is decided.** Every date and plan on this page is a proposal. The whole schedule also depends on the site host approving the location. Tell us what you think: see [how to have your say](docs/have-your-say.md).

## The proposed timeline at a glance

**You can contribute right now.** Nothing holds that back: no gates and no dates. The dates below are for meetups, events, and decisions. The first meetup is Wednesday, January 13, 2027 (proposed), and until then we are gathering people while contributions come in.

| Phase | Proposed dates | What happens | How you can help |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Gather people | Now to Wed Jan 13, 2027 | We collect people and recruit the founding team. Contributions are open. No events yet | [Count yourself in](https://github.com/Fishers-Founders-United/Swapatron5000/issues/new?template=count-me-in.yml), or [apply to the founding team](docs/founding-team.md) |
| 1. Listen and contribute | Now to Sun Feb 21, 2027 (first meetup Wed Jan 13) | We collect ideas, sketches, and concerns | Share a sketch, answer an open question, tell a friend |
| 2. Decide | Mon Feb 22 to Sun Mar 14, 2027 | We summarize what we heard, propose decisions, and take final comments | Read the summary and comment |
| 3. Gather materials | Mon Mar 15 to Sun Apr 25, 2027 | We collect every item on the wishlist | Pledge or drop off materials |
| 4. Build | Sat May 1, 2027 | Build day, indoors at the lab | Come and build |
| 5. Install and open | Sat May 15 (install), Sat Jun 5, 2027 (opening) | The box goes in the ground and opens | Stock it, join the upkeep rota |
| 6. Look after it | Every week from Jun 5, 2027 | Recurring upkeep | Take a week on the rota |
| 7. Make it smart | Any time after opening | Power, light, sensors, software | Join a team |

## Dates and events

The first meetup is on a Wednesday evening, proposed for the Mutiny19 Discord. No events are planned before it. Contributing online is open the whole time.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Wed Jan 13, 2027 | **Meet Swapatron**: the first meetup, the founding team kickoff, and the start of everything. Nothing is built yet |
| Sat Jan 30, 2027 | Draw Swapatron: sketch day for all ages |
| Thu Feb 18, 2027 | "This won't work because..." night |
| Thu Mar 4, 2027 | Decision night |
| Sat Apr 10, 2027 | Feed Swapatron: parts drive and kit making |
| Sat May 1, 2027 | Build day |
| Sat May 15, 2027 | Install day |
| Sat Jun 5, 2027 | Hatching day: Swapatron opens, with food trucks, a celebration, and a technology petting zoo |
| Sat Sep 11, 2027 | 90 day check-up |

Every event is a proposal until the lab confirms it. Details, and who each event is for: [launch dates and events](docs/events.md).

Why this long? We start in January, and then listening, deciding, gathering, and building take about four months. Posts can not be set in frozen ground, and a June opening means warm weather for hatching day and the technology petting zoo. A faster opening is not realistic.

**Goal:** a room full of people on the first night.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| Now | Set up: create the issues and labels, name interim maintainers, ask the lab for site approval |
| Now to Sun Jan 10 | The [founding team](docs/founding-team.md) call is open. Applications and replies happen in writing, on GitHub |
| Now to Wed Jan 13 | People [count themselves in](https://github.com/Fishers-Founders-United/Swapatron5000/issues/new?template=count-me-in.yml). Invite friends, makers, teachers, and neighbors, using the [share kit](docs/share-kit.md) |
| Sun Jan 10 | The founding team is named |
| Wed Jan 13 | **Meet Swapatron.** The first meetup, proposed for the Mutiny19 Discord. The time is set and the invite link is posted by then |

No meetings and no events are planned before Jan 13. Contributing is open now: [six doors](docs/start-here.md), including drawings, UI and UX, IoT integration briefs, asks, and the kit wishlist.

## Phase 1: Listen and contribute (now to Feb 21, 2027)

**Goal:** hear from as many people as possible before anything is chosen.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| Now | Contributions are open. Sketches, briefs, asks, and ideas are all welcome |
| Wed Jan 13 | Meet Swapatron: the first meetup |
| Sat Jan 30 | Draw Swapatron: sketch day |
| Thu Feb 18 | "This won't work because..." night |
| Sun Feb 21 | Listening closes |

That is about three months of listening, with the first meetup in the middle. If turnout is thin, the close date should move back, and every later date with it.

- Design sketches are open
- Open questions are posted as issues (see [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md))
- Site approval is requested from the lab and the property owner

**Closes:** Sunday, February 21, 2027. Ideas that arrive later are still welcome, but may miss the first box.

## Phase 2: Decide (Feb 22 to Mar 14, 2027)

**Goal:** turn what we heard into a plan, in the open.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| Feb 22 to Feb 28 | Maintainers post a summary of every open question and a proposed answer for each |
| Mar 1 to Mar 14 | Final comment period. Anyone can object or suggest changes. Decision night on Thu Mar 4 |
| By Sun Mar 14 | Decisions are recorded in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) with the reasons |

## Phase 3: Gather materials (Mar 15 to Apr 25, 2027)

**Goal:** every item on the wishlist is marked `received`.

| Dates | Step |
|---|---|
| By Sun Mar 21 | Cut list and final wishlist published from the chosen design |
| Mar 22 to Apr 25 | Pledges and drop-offs. Feed Swapatron parts drive on Sat Apr 10 |
| Sun Apr 25 | Materials check. If key items are missing, build day moves |

Supplies that any design will need, such as anti-static bags and desiccant, can be pledged right now.

## Phase 4: Build day (Sat May 1, 2027)

**Goal:** a finished box, built together, indoors. See the [build day plan](docs/build-day.md).

## Phase 5: Install and open (May and June 2027)

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| Week of May 10 | Call 811 to have underground lines marked before any digging |
| Sat May 15 | Install day. Backup date: Sat May 22 |
| Sat Jun 5 | Opening day, with food trucks, a celebration, and a technology petting zoo. The box is stocked and the rota starts |

## Phase 6: Look after it (from Jun 5, 2027)

Weekly, monthly, and seasonal upkeep. See the [maintenance schedule](docs/maintenance.md).

**Sat Sep 11, 2027:** 90 day review. What worked, what broke, what to change.

## Phase 7: Make it smart (after opening)

Power, lighting, sensors, alerts, inventory, and any lock. Planning can start any time, but the box should work with no electronics first.

Good. Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) and say which date should move and why.
