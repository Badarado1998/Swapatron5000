# UI and UX team

We make the box easy to find, easy to use, and easy to understand. You do not need to code. If you can notice when something is confusing, you can do this work.

## The people we design for

| Person | What they need |
|---|---|
| A curious kid | To know in five seconds what this is and that they are allowed to use it |
| A passerby | To know what they can take and what they can leave |
| A maker with spare parts | To know what is wanted and how to bag and label it |
| A first time GitHub user | To make a first contribution without feeling lost |
| A steward | To know quickly whether the box needs attention |

## Principles

- Plain words. Short sentences.
- Pictures and icons, so young kids and people who do not read English well can use the box.
- Good contrast and large type.
- Works on a phone, outdoors, in sunlight.

## Open questions

1. What is the one sentence on the sign that makes someone stop?
2. What should a person see first when they scan the QR code?
3. How do we show "tested working" versus "untested" at a glance?

## How to contribute

- Share a sketch, a mockup, or a photo of a drawing in an [idea](../../../issues/new?template=idea.yml).
- Put finished artwork in `ux/` in a pull request, with an editable source file.
- Test with real people. Hand the sign to someone who has never heard of the project and write down what confuses them. That is a contribution.
