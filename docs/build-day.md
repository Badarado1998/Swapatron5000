# Build day plan (proposal)

> **Nothing here is decided.** This is a draft plan for comment. The date depends on site approval, the chosen design, and materials arriving on time.

**Proposed date:** Saturday, March 20, 2027
**Proposed place:** indoors at the Indiana IoT Lab, 9059 Technology Lane, Fishers, Indiana (to be confirmed with the lab)

Building indoors means the weather can not cancel it. The finished box is installed outside later, once the ground has thawed.

## Before build day

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|

| By Sun Mar 14 | All materials at the lab and checked against the wishlist | Needs owner |
| By Sun Mar 14 | Tool list confirmed: who is bringing what | Needs owner |

## Proposed schedule for the day

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:00 | Set up, coffee, safety talk |
| 9:30 | Team 1 builds the shell. Team 2 builds the roof. Team 3 preps bins and labels |
| 11:30 | Fit the door, hinges, latch, and vents |
| 12:30 | Lunch |
| 1:15 | Seal and paint. Mount the sign |
| 2:30 | Kids' table: decorate bin labels, bag and label starter parts |
| 3:30 | Fit the bins, check the box against the design brief, take photos |

## Jobs for every skill level

| Job | Good for |
|---|---|
| Measuring, drilling, and assembly | Adults and teens comfortable with hand tools |
| Sanding and painting | Anyone, including supervised kids |
| Bagging and labeling starter parts | Kids and newcomers |
| Photos and notes for the build guide | Anyone with a phone |
| Updating the wishlist and Contributors Wall | Anyone who wants GitHub practice |

## Safety on the day

- Safety glasses for anyone near cutting or drilling
- Power tools are for adults, or teens with an adult beside them
- Children need a parent or guardian with them
- Closed-toe shoes
