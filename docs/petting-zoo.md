# The Technology Petting Zoo

**Proposed for hatching day: a hands-on zoo of friendly gadgets, for adults and their understudies (kids).**

When Swapatron hatches, we would like to open with a celebration, food trucks, and a place where anyone can walk up to a gadget and poke it. No experience needed. The only rule is "pet gently."

> **Nothing is decided yet.** The date, the place, the food trucks, and every station below are proposals. Hatching day is proposed for Sat Apr 24, 2027, and nothing is confirmed with the lab. See [events](events.md) and [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).

New to the lingo? [Swapatron-speak](swapatron-speak.md) translates every funny word into plain English.

## What it is

A set of tables, each one a "habitat" with a few friendly critters (small working gadgets) and a keeper (a volunteer) who explains them. Visitors press, blow on, wave at, and talk to the critters, and see what happens. Understudies (kids) go with an adult, and everyone goes home knowing one more thing about how the connected world works.

## The habitats (all proposed)

| Habitat | What lives there | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **The Gate** | A welcome table and a sign | Meet a keeper, hear the pet-gently rules, get a map |
| **Blinky Meadow** | LEDs and light strips | Press a button and change the colors |
| **Sensor Pen** | Temperature, humidity, light, and soil moisture sensors | Breathe on one and watch the number climb |
| **Ping Pond** | Two small boards talking to each other | Send a "ping" and see the little letter (packet) arrive |
| **Mesh Burrow** | A few small radios passing a message along, like a herd | Send a short text from one end and watch it hop across |
| **Heartbeat Corner** | A node that says "I'm alive" every few seconds | Watch the heartbeat, then unplug it and see what happens |
| **Watchdog Kennel** | A device that restarts itself when it gets stuck | Make it freeze and watch the watchdog bark it awake |
| **Servo Stables** | Small motors, with finger guards | Turn a knob and make a little arm wave |
| **Beep Barn** | Buzzers, speakers, and tiny screens | Make a noise, then make it a tune |
| **Critter Clinic** | A bench and a keeper with spare cables | Bring a stuck board and get a second pair of eyes. No promise to fix it |
| **Feeding Time** | Swapatron itself | The first feeding. Bring a part, take a part |

Not every habitat will happen. Which ones exist depends on who lends a critter and who keeps a habitat.

## Be a keeper, or lend a critter

- **Lend a critter.** Bring a gadget you built or own and show it off. You stay with it the whole time. [Lend a critter](../../../issues/new?template=lend-a-critter.yml).
- **Keep a habitat.** Run one table for a stretch of the day. Take the "zookeeper" bounty on the [bounty board](../BOUNTIES.md).
- **Run the whole zoo.** Take the "technology petting zoo" role on the [founding team](founding-team.md).

## The pet-gently rules (proposed)

Rules for the zoo are serious even when the names are silly. These need review before the day, and the youth rules depend on [Decision 18](../DECISIONS.md).

1. **Low voltage only.** Gadgets run from USB-level power. No mains voltage is exposed anywhere in the zoo.
2. **No loose lithium cells, and no charging.** Batteries stay sealed inside their gadgets.
3. **No hot tools and no sharp tools.** No soldering irons, hot air guns, knives, or blades at the zoo.
4. **Owners stay with their critters.** Nobody leaves a gadget alone on a table.
5. **Understudies stay with an adult.** Every child comes with a parent or guardian who stays with them.
6. **No adult is alone with someone else's child.** Keepers work in pairs, or in view of other people.
7. **No food or drink at the tables.** Food trucks are for eating. Crumbs and spills kill electronics.
8. **Ask before you take a photo of a person.** Never post a photo of a child without a parent's yes.
9. **No personal data.** Demos must not ask for names, emails, or phone numbers, and must not record faces or voices.
10. **Radios are legal and polite.** Anyone lending a gadget that transmits says what it does, and follows the local rules for it.
11. **Keepers can say stop.** If a gadget is getting hot, smells odd, or is being treated roughly, a keeper turns it off first and talks second.

## What we have to check before we promise anything

- The site host agrees to a public event, outlets, tables, and the zoo
- Whether the host's insurance covers an event with visitors, and what the city of Fishers requires
- How many tables, outlets, and keepers we really have
- Whether the Mesh Burrow can run without bothering anyone else's radios
- A rain plan, since it is April in Indiana
- A short test run in the week before, where every critter and every rule is tried out

## Open questions
