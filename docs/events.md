# Launch dates and events

> **Every date here is a proposal.** None of these events is confirmed with the Indiana IoT Lab yet. Times will be added when they are. Please do not travel for an in-person event until its row says "Confirmed".

**Everything before 2027 happens online.** Nothing in person is planned until January 2027. Online events are on a video call, and the link will be posted on the event's issue once it is confirmed.

In-person events, from January 2027, are proposed for the Indiana IoT Lab, 9059 Technology Lane, Fishers, Indiana. All events are free, and you do not need to know anything to come.

| If you are... | Start with |

| Curious, and new to all of this | Meet Swapatron, or Draw Swapatron |
| A kid, or bringing one | Draw Swapatron, Build day, Hatching day and the [technology petting zoo](petting-zoo.md) |

## How to say you are coming

Each event will have its own issue once it is confirmed. Give it a thumbs up to say you are coming, so we know how many chairs to put out. [How to do that](have-your-say.md).

To hear when events are confirmed, tap **Watch** at the top of the project page.

## At every event

- A help desk for making your first GitHub contribution, with a person, not a manual
- Children are welcome with a parent or guardian

## At in-person events (from January 2027)

- Design sheets and pencils
- A place to drop off parts for the box, once the lab confirms drop-offs

## At online events

- Children join with a parent or guardian, and the adult stays in the room
- Cameras are optional, and nobody has to speak
- No recording of children, and no private messages between adults and children
- Questions go in the open chat, where everyone can see them
- Ask before you screenshot or share anyone's face

These are proposals. See [Decision 18](../DECISIONS.md).

## Want to host or help at one?

Open an [idea](../../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) with the title "Event: " and say which one. Running an event earns bounty points. See [BOUNTIES.md](../BOUNTIES.md).

## For maintainers

- Confirm each date and a time with the lab before changing its status to "Confirmed".
- Create one issue per confirmed event, with the `event` label, and link it from the table.
- Do not promote an event publicly while it says "Proposed".
- If a date moves, change it here first. Then update [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md), the README, and the web page.

## About hatching day

The plan is a celebration with food trucks when Swapatron opens. It is a plan, not a booking. Before anyone promises it publicly, someone has to check:

- The site host agrees to a public event and to trucks on its property
- What the city of Fishers requires for a gathering, for vendors, and for parking
- Who invites the trucks, and who pays for anything. No one person should end up with the bill
- Where people park, and where the line forms, so the box and the doors stay clear
- A rain plan, because it is April in Indiana
- Who thanks the people on the [Contributors Wall](../CONTRIBUTORS.md), by name, out loud

Want to run it? Take the hatching day bounty on [the bounty board](../BOUNTIES.md).
