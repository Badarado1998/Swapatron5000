# Start here: six open doors

**Pick a door. Walk through it. Nobody needs to ask permission.**

This project is a framework, not a finished plan. Almost nothing is built, and that is the point: the doors below are open so you can start. Every door has a first step you can finish in about fifteen minutes.

> **Nothing is decided yet.** Everything you start here is a proposal that others can improve. See [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md). These doors are open now, with no gates and no dates. The dates in the plan are for meetups and decisions, not for contributing.

## The six doors

| Door | What you do | First 15 minutes | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Point your AI at it** | Let your AI assistant read the project and help you contribute | Paste the one-line prompt from [ai-contribute.md](ai-contribute.md) into your AI, and tell it what you are good at | [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) |
| **2. Add to the ask list** | Say what you need for a project, or fill someone's ask | Open [Ask for a part](../../../issues/new?template=ask-for-a-part.yml) and ask for one thing | [ASKS.md](../ASKS.md) |
| **3. Start a drawing** | Draw the box, a part of it, or the site | Pick a view from [the wanted list](../hardware/designs/WANTED.md) and sketch it on paper | [hardware/designs/](../hardware/designs/README.md) |
| **4. Start UI and UX** | Design how people find, use, and understand the box | Pick a screen from [the wanted list](../ux/WANTED.md) and sketch it | [ux/](../ux/README.md) |
| **5. Design an IoT integration** | Plan how cameras, inventory, solar, safety, security, and discovery could work | Pick a track in [integrations/](../integrations/README.md) and write down your first idea | [integrations/](../integrations/README.md) |
| **6. Fill the kit wishlist** | Pledge or lend the parts that contributors need to prototype | Find one item in [the kit wishlist](../bom/KITS.md) you could give or lend | [bom/KITS.md](../bom/KITS.md) |

Each door has a claim thread on the Issues tab. Comment there to say what you are taking, so nobody does the same thing twice. More than one person can work on the same thing, and that is fine.

## The same four steps at every door

1. **Look.** Read the "wanted" list for the door.
2. **Say.** Open a [brief](../../../issues/new?template=start-a-brief.yml) or comment on the claim thread, with one sentence about what you will do.
3. **Make a small thing.** A sketch, a paragraph, a list, a rough idea. Small is good.
4. **Share it.** Post it as an issue or a pull request. Others improve it, and you go on the [Contributors Wall](../CONTRIBUTORS.md).

## You do not have to build any of it

These doors are for **starting**. Nobody is asking you to finish the camera system or draw a perfect box. A good first contribution is a clear question, a rough sketch, or a list of what could go wrong.

## Safety stays on

Anything with a battery, mains power, a lock, a camera, children, or structure needs a qualified human to review it before it is built. That is true for AI-assisted work too. See the [proposed safety baseline](../DECISIONS.md).

## Stuck?

Open a "Help" issue. A keeper will walk you through it, and nobody will mind. New to GitHub? [This guide](have-your-say.md) shows where to tap.
