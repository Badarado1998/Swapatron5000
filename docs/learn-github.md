# Learn GitHub in 15 minutes

GitHub is a website where people work on projects together. Every change is saved, so nothing you do here can break the project. If you make a mistake, a maintainer will help you fix it.

Everything below works in a web browser, on a phone or a computer.

Only want to comment or vote? [How to have your say](have-your-say.md) is the two minute version.

## Five words you will see

- **Repository (repo):** the project folder. You are in it now.
- **Issue:** a note on the to-do list. It can be a task, a question, an idea, or an offer to donate a part.
- **Commit:** one saved change.
- **Pull request (PR):** "Here is my change. Please review it and add it."
- **Merge:** a maintainer accepts your pull request and it becomes part of the project.

## Step 1: Make an account

Go to github.com and sign up. It is free. GitHub requires users to be at least 13. Younger makers can take part with a parent, guardian, or teacher using the adult's account.

## Step 2: Your first contribution (no code)

1. Open the **Issues** tab at the top of this repository.
2. Click **New issue**.
3. Choose **Pledge a part** or **Idea**.
4. Fill in the form and click **Submit new issue**.

That is a real open source contribution.

## Step 3: Your first edit

1. Open [bom/BOM.csv](../bom/BOM.csv).
2. Click the pencil icon in the top right of the file.
3. GitHub will offer to "fork" the repository. Say yes. A fork is your own copy, so you can not damage the original.
4. Change one line. For example, add your username to `pledged_by` and raise `qty_pledged`.
5. Click **Commit changes**, then **Propose changes**, then **Create pull request**.
6. A maintainer will review it. They may ask a question or suggest a tweak. That is normal and friendly.

## Stuck?

Open an issue with the title "Help: " and describe what you tried. Nobody here will mind. Come to a build day at the lab and someone will walk you through it in person.
