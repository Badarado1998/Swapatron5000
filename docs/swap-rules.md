# Swap rules (draft)

> **Draft. Nothing here is decided.** This page is a starting point for discussion. See [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md) and [how to have your say](have-your-say.md).

## The short version (for the sign)

Take parts. Leave parts. IoT and electronics only.

## Welcome

- Working dev boards and microcontrollers
- Sensors, displays, and modules
- Wire, jumpers, connectors, headers, and cables
- Resistors, capacitors, LEDs, and other small components
- Breadboards, enclosures, and mounting hardware

## Not welcome

- Loose lithium batteries of any kind, or devices with swollen or damaged batteries
- Anything that plugs into a wall outlet
- Broken or untested gear
- Soldering irons, blades, and other sharp or hot tools
- Chemicals, flux, and solder
- Anything that is not electronics

## How to leave a part

1. Test it if you can.
2. Put boards and chips in an anti-static bag. Put small parts in a zip bag.
3. Label the bag: what it is, and "tested working" or "untested".
4. Boards, sensors, and chips go in the sealed bin. Close the lid firmly. Everything else goes in the open bins.

## Good to know

- Parts are free and come with no guarantee. Use them at your own risk.
- Small parts are a choking hazard. Young children need an adult with them.
- Take what you will use. Leave some for the next person.
- If the box is damaged, wet inside, or out of bags, scan the QR code and open an issue.
