# Hatch your own Swapatron

**Box one is planned for Fishers, Indiana. Swapatron is not only for Fishers.**

Any town, school, library, makerspace, scout troop, or club can hatch its own. The plans, the rules, the wall, and the tutorials are open, and every box makes the next one easier to build.

> **Nothing is decided yet.** How boxes join the network is an open question ([Decision 17](../DECISIONS.md)). Everything below is a proposal, so tell us what you would change.

## Should you wait for box one?

You do not have to wait to start planning. Be honest with yourself about the risk, though:

- Box one has no tested design yet. The drawings are examples to react to.
- The safety baseline in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md) is a proposal that still needs expert review.
- If you build before a design is chosen, you are the test. That is welcome, as long as you share what breaks.

Many people will want to wait for the Fishers build day in March 2027 and copy what works. Both are fine.

## What you get

- Every design, drawing, and wishlist in this repository
- The [swap rules](swap-rules.md), the [upkeep plan](maintenance.md), and the [build day plan](build-day.md), ready to copy and edit
- The [Contributors Wall](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) approach, so your helpers get celebrated too
- A place to ask questions, and other box keepers to ask them of
- Share-alike [licenses](../LICENSE.md), so what you build stays open for the next town

## What we ask (proposed)

1. **Get the site host's written yes.** A box on someone's property is their call. They can veto anything.
2. **Name a steward, and a second person.** A box with nobody looking after it becomes a junk pile.
3. **Follow the safety baseline and the swap rules.** No mains power, no loose lithium cells, no battery charging, no used battery collection. If you want to change one, open an `rfc` issue and explain why.
4. **Keep it free.** No selling parts, no accounts required to take a part, and no personal data collected by default.
5. **Share back.** Send your improvements and lessons back so the next builder starts from your version. The licenses require this for the designs and code anyway.
6. **Use the name kindly.** Call your box a Swapatron if it follows these rules. See [The name and the creature](../LICENSE.md).

## Local rules are yours to check

Towns, property owners, schools, and insurers have different rules about structures, signs, and giving things away. We can not tell you what applies where you live. Ask your site host, and check with your town if the box would stand on public land.

## How to hatch one

1. **Read** the [README](../README.md) and the [open questions](../DECISIONS.md) so you know what is still unsettled.
2. **Find a host** and a steward. A library, a makerspace, a school, a business, or a neighbor with a front yard can all work.
3. **Tell us.** [Open a "New box" form](../../../issues/new?template=hatch-a-swapatron.yml). Give a town and a state or country, not a street address.
4. **Fork or copy** the designs and adapt them to your climate. Indiana winters are not everyone's winters.
5. **Run your own wishlist and asks.** Each box needs its own `bom/` list and its own drop-off point. Parts asked for in one town are collected in that town.
6. **Come back and tell us how it went.** Your first month will teach everyone more than any plan.

## What a network could do later

These are dreams, not plans. See [VISION.md](../VISION.md).

- A map of every box and what each one needs
- Shared designs that improve with every build
- Boxes lending each other ideas, and sometimes parts
- One shared pool of lessons, so a mistake in one town is made only once

## Questions we have not answered

- Who keeps the list of boxes, and how does a box leave it?
- What happens when a box goes quiet?
- Should boxes agree on one standard module size, so parts fit everywhere?
- Does a box in another country need different safety rules?
- How do boxes share the Contributors Wall points?
