# How to have your say

You do not need to code. You do not need to install anything. This works on a phone.

Nothing about the box is decided yet, so your opinion counts. Here are four ways to give it, from easiest to most involved.

## 1. Vote with a thumbs up (30 seconds)

1. Tap the **Issues** tab at the top of the project page.
2. Tap a question that interests you.
3. Find a comment you agree with.
4. Tap the smiley face on that comment and choose the thumbs up.

That is a vote. Maintainers count them.

## 2. Leave a comment (2 minutes)

1. Open an issue from the **Issues** tab.
2. Scroll to the box at the bottom that says "Add a comment".
3. Type what you think. Plain words are perfect.
4. Tap **Comment**.

Good comments say what you would choose and why. "I'd pick the wall mount because the snow plow piles up by the curb" is more useful than "wall mount".

## 3. Share a sketch or an idea (10 minutes)

1. Tap the **Issues** tab, then **New issue**.
2. Choose **Share a design sketch** or **Idea**.
3. Fill in the form. To add a photo, tap inside the big text box and attach or paste the picture.
4. Tap **Submit new issue**.

## 4. Ask a question (2 minutes)

Not sure where something goes? Choose **Help, I am stuck** from the **New issue** list. Nobody will mind.

## What you need first

A free GitHub account. Go to github.com and tap **Sign up**. You need an email address. GitHub requires users to be at least 13, so younger makers can take part with a parent, guardian, or teacher using the adult's account.

## What happens to my comment?

- A maintainer reads every one.
- When the listening phase closes on January 10, 2027, maintainers post a summary of what people said and a proposed answer for each question.
- You then get until January 31 to object or suggest changes.
- Final decisions and the reasons are written in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).
