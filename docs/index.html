Free electronics parts swap box

IoT Swapatron 5000

A community plan for a free parts box. Take a part. Leave a part. Dev boards, wire, sensors, and connectors, free for anyone who wants to build something.

Nothing is decided yet

This box does not exist yet. We are collecting ideas until Sunday, January 10, 2027. The design, the rules, and the timeline are all open, and we want your opinion.

Have your say

Read the dream, and add your own. Proposed plan: build day on Saturday, March 20, 2027, and opening day on Saturday, April 24, 2027. See the timeline.

The idea

  1. Open the box and take what you will use.
  2. Leave your spare parts for the next person.
  3. Bag it and label it: what it is, and whether you tested it.

That is the plan so far. Boards, sensors, and chips would go in a sealed bin, and everything else in open bins.

What might go in the box (draft)

Yes please

  • Working dev boards
  • Sensors and displays
  • Wire, jumpers, and cables
  • Connectors and headers
  • Small components
  • Breadboards and enclosures

No thanks

  • Loose lithium batteries
  • Anything that plugs into a wall
  • Broken gear
  • Sharp or hot tools
  • Chemicals, flux, and solder

Parts are free and come with no guarantee. Small parts are a choking hazard, so young children need an adult with them.
