# Join the founding team

**Swapatron needs keepers. We are looking for the first group of people who will help it hatch.**

Box one, in Fishers, is the HQ model and the primary test bed. The founding team builds it first, learns from it, and writes down everything so other towns can copy what works.

The founding team is a small group of volunteers who each look after one part of the project. You do not need to be an expert, and you do not need to know GitHub yet. You need to care about getting free parts into the hands of people who will build with them, and to turn up.

> **Nothing is decided yet.** The roles, the time asks, and the dates on this page are proposals. Tell us what you would change. See [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).

## What you would get

- Your name and photo on the [Contributors Wall](../CONTRIBUTORS.md), and a thank-you out loud on hatching day
- A real say in how Swapatron works, because decisions are made in the open and you help make them
- A buddy if you are new to something. Nobody figures it out alone
- The thanks of everyone who uses the box. This is volunteer work and nobody is paid

## What we ask of you (proposed)

1. Be 18 or older. Kids are welcome as understudies, with an adult. See [Decision 18](../DECISIONS.md)
2. Agree to the [code of conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
3. Do what you said you would do, or say early that you can not
4. Work in the open, so others can see and join in
5. Agree the work is share-alike. See [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md)
6. Step aside gracefully when your time is up. Every role is reviewed at the 90 day check-up on Sat Sep 11, 2027

## How to apply

1. [Open the founding team form](../../../issues/new?template=join-founding-team.yml). It takes about five minutes
2. Pick a role, or two. "Not sure" is a fine answer
3. Do not post your address, phone number, or email. We will reply in the issue, and set up a chat from there
4. We will talk with you, in the open or in a small group, before the call closes
5. The team is announced with reasons

Never used GitHub? [This two minute guide](have-your-say.md) shows you where to tap. If you get stuck, open a "Help" issue and a keeper will walk you through it.

## Under 18?

You are an understudy, and we want you. Understudies design, build, test, and name things, and their names go on the wall too. An adult comes with you, and applies for the team with you if you would like to be on it. See the [petting zoo](petting-zoo.md) and [Draw Swapatron](events.md).

## How the team picks itself (proposed)

- Everyone who applies gets an answer, even if it is "not yet"
- Maintainers and the site host talk with each applicant, and choose openly
- No one is chosen for a role they have not asked for
- We aim for a mix: beginners and veterans, Fishers locals and remote helpers, and more than one age group
- Two people per role is better than one, because people get busy
