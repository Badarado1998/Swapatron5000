## Please keep these true when you share

- **Say "planning" or "proposed".** The box is not built, and the site is not approved yet.
- **Contributing is open now.** There are no gates and no dates. But no events are planned before Wed Jan 13, 2027, so never invite anyone to an event or to the lab, or ask for parts to be dropped off, before the lab confirms.
- **The first meetup has nothing built to see.** Say so. People should come to meet the idea and the people, not to look at a box. It is proposed to be online, on the Mutiny19 Discord. Do not share an invite link until a maintainer adds it to [events.md](events.md).
- **Do not promise dates.** The dates are proposals. Only promote an event once [events.md](events.md) says it is confirmed.
- **Say it is not only Fishers.** Box one is in Fishers. Any town can hatch its own. See [your-swapatron.md](your-swapatron.md).
- **"Born out of" is not "approved by".** The project was born out of Indiana IoT and Fishers Founders United, with Mutiny19. Do not say the lab or anyone else has approved or endorsed it until they have.
- **Do not use the lab's logo** or say the lab endorses the project unless the lab has agreed.
- **Do not share the shipping address** until the notice in [ASKS.md](../ASKS.md) is removed.

## Before the project is announced widely

A short checklist for the maintainers. Announce in this order.

- [ ] The lab has agreed to the project being announced with its name attached
- [ ] The open questions exist as issues, so visitors have somewhere to comment
- [ ] The `ask`, `bounty`, `decision`, and `challenge` labels exist
- [ ] At least two maintainers are named in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md)
- [ ] Three or four people have each posted a first sketch or comment, so the project does not look empty
- [ ] The web page and every button on it have been tested on a phone
- [ ] Someone is watching the issues daily for the first two weeks, to welcome people and remove personal details
