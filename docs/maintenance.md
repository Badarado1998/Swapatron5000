# Maintenance schedule (draft)

## Weekly check (about 10 minutes)

- Remove anything on the "not welcome" list, especially batteries
- Remove wet, broken, or unlabeled junk
- Check that the sealed bin lid is closed and the gasket is seated
- Look at the desiccant indicator color
- Restock anti-static bags, zip bags, and labels
- Wipe the door panel and check the sign is readable

## Monthly

- Replace or recharge desiccant
- Check hinges, latch, vents, and roof for leaks or damage
- Check battery, solar panel, and lighting (once installed)
- Update the Bill of Materials for used-up supplies

## Twice a year (spring and fall)

- Inspect sealant, paint, and gaskets. Reseal if needed
- Check the mount for movement or rot
- Review the sensor log for humidity trends (once installed)
