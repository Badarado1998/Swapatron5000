## How to claim one

Comment on the drawing's claim thread on the Issues tab, or just post your sketch. You do not have to ask first.

## What a good drawing includes

- A title and your GitHub name
- Rough sizes, marked as guesses where they are guesses
- One sentence on why you drew it that way
- What you were unsure about

## Where finished drawings go

Photos and sketches stay on their issue. Drawings that become files go in [submissions/](submissions/README.md).
