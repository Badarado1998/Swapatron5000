# Code of conduct

> **Draft. Nothing here is decided.** This is a starting point, written in plain words, and the community can change it. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

Swapatron is a place for people who are curious about building things. Children use it, and so do beginners who are nervous about asking. This page is about making it safe and friendly for all of them.

## What we expect

- **Be kind.** Disagree with an idea, never with a person's worth.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Everyone began somewhere. "How do I..." is always a good question.
- **Say it plainly.** Skip the jargon, or explain it.
- **Assume good intent, and check.** If something sounds rude, ask what was meant before you reply.
- **Keep kids safe.** See the rules below.
- **Respect the host.** The lab or any site host is lending us a space.

Doubts and criticism are welcome. "This won't work because..." is a contribution. See [CHALLENGES.md](CHALLENGES.md). Being unkind about a person is not.

## What is not okay

- Insults, harassment, or threats, in any form
- Targeting someone for who they are
- Sharing someone's private information
- Sexual content, or any contact with a child that an adult would not want a parent to read
- Spam, sales pitches, and trying to take credit for others' work
- Pretending to speak for the project, the lab, or any person

## Kids and understudies

- Children use this project with a parent or guardian. Do not ask a child for personal details, a photo, or a private conversation.
- Do not message a child privately. Keep every conversation in the open, where others can see it.
- No adult is alone with someone else's child at an event. Keepers work in pairs or in view of others.
- Never post a photo of a child without a parent's yes.
- If you are worried about a child's safety, tell a maintainer or the event lead right away, and contact the right authorities if a child may be in danger.

The detailed rules are a proposal. See [Decision 18](DECISIONS.md).

## When something goes wrong

- **Online:** use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or ask any maintainer to hide or remove it. Maintainers can edit, hide, or delete comments, and can block someone from the repository.
- **At an event:** tell any keeper or the event lead. They can ask someone to leave.
- **A private way to report:** a private contact for conduct reports has not been set up yet. Setting one up is one of the first jobs for the founding team.

Reports are taken seriously, handled by at least two maintainers where possible, and kept as private as the situation allows. Nobody is punished for making a report in good faith.

## What happens next

Maintainers may warn, ask for a change, hide content, or remove someone from the project. The response matches the harm, and the reasons are explained to the person involved.

## Where this applies

Everywhere Swapatron shows up: this repository, its web page, its events, and any chat or social space run in its name.
