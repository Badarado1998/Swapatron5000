# Bounties

> **This is a proposal.** The bounty list, the points, and the levels are all open for comment. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

A bounty is a clearly described job with points attached. Finish it, and the points go next to your name on the [Contributors Wall](CONTRIBUTORS.md).

Points are for fun and for thanks. They are not money, and nobody is ranked by how much they spend.

## How it works

1. **Find one.** Browse issues labeled [bounty](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Abounty), or the table below.
2. **Claim it.** Comment "I'll take this" on the issue. A maintainer assigns it to you.
3. **Do it.** Ask questions in the issue as you go. Teams are welcome and share the points.
4. **Hand it in.** Post your work in the issue, or open a pull request.
5. **Collect.** When a maintainer accepts it, your points go on the wall.

A claim lasts two weeks. If you need longer, say so. If a claim goes quiet, the bounty reopens for someone else.

New to GitHub? [How to have your say](docs/have-your-say.md) shows you how to comment on an issue.

## Points

| Size | Points | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Quick win | 1 | Under 30 minutes. A pledge, a sketch, a weekly box check |
| Small | 2 | An evening |
| Medium | 3 | A weekend |
| Large | 5 | Several weekends, or work that others depend on |

Giving a part is always 1 point, whatever it cost. A bag of jumper wires counts the same as a solar panel.

## Levels

| Level | Points | |
|---|---|---|
| Spark | 1 | You made your first contribution |
| Tinkerer | 5 | You keep coming back |
| Builder | 15 | The box has your fingerprints on it |
| Architect | 30 | The box would not exist without you |

## Suggest a bounty

Know a job that needs doing? Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) with the title "Bounty: " and describe the job and what "done" looks like.

## For maintainers

- A good bounty says exactly what "done" means, so nobody has to guess.
- Add the `bounty` label and put the points in the title, for example "Bounty (2): Design the sign".
- When you accept the work, add a line to `contributions.csv` with the points. The wall rebuilds itself.
- Rewards beyond points, such as stickers or names on a plaque on the box, are a good idea if someone offers to fund them. Do not promise them until they exist.
