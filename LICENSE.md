## The short version

**Build your own Swapatron. Improve it. Share it. Just do not close it up or pass it off as yours alone.**

Every license here is a "share-alike" license. That means:

- **You may** copy, build, change, and share everything in this project, for free, including commercially.
- **You must** say where it came from. Credit "the IoT Swapatron 5000 contributors" and link back to this repository.
- **You must** share your changes under the same license. If you improve the box, the code, or the docs and pass them on, everyone gets your improvements too.
- **You may not** take this work, lock it down, and claim it as your own.

That last point is the reason for this choice. The goal is one shared pool of designs that gets better with every box anyone builds, not a hundred closed copies.

## Contributing

When you contribute, you keep the copyright to your work, and you agree to share it under the license that covers that kind of work in the table above. You also confirm that it is yours to share.

## The name and the creature

Licenses cover the work. They do not cover the name. "Swapatron", "IoT Swapatron 5000", and the Swapatron character identify this community project.

- **Please do** call your box a Swapatron if it follows the [swap rules](docs/swap-rules.md), stays free to use, and is listed with the project so others can find it.
- **Please do not** use the name for a box that sells parts, for a closed copy, or in a way that suggests the project endorses something it has not.

The name policy is a proposal and is not yet backed by a registered trademark.

## One network, many boxes

The dream is a single, shared Swapatron family: many boxes in many towns, all drawing on and adding to the same designs. If you build one:

1. Tell us. Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) with the title "New box: " and where it is.
2. Share what you changed and what you learned.
3. Send your improvements back as pull requests, so the next builder starts from your version.

## What a license can not do

To be honest about the limits:

- It can not stop someone from building a similar box from their own design. Ideas are free.
- It relies on people following it. Enforcing it would take effort, and possibly a lawyer.
- It does not make anyone liable for a box they did not build. Everything here is provided as is, with no warranty.
