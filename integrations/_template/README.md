# Integration brief template

Copy this into a new folder, or paste it into an issue.

**Track:**
**By:** (GitHub username)
**AI-assisted:** yes or no
**Status:** Idea, Brief, RFC, Prototype, Review, or Adopted

## Goal
What problem does this solve, in one or two sentences?

## Who benefits?
(A visitor, a steward, a kid, another town)

## Not goals
What this is not trying to do.

## The simplest version
What is the cheapest, smallest thing that would help? Could it be done with no electronics at all?

## Risks
Safety, privacy, security, cost, upkeep. Be blunt.

## Does it need an `rfc`?
Yes, if it involves a camera, a microphone, a lock, a battery, or anything collecting personal data.

## Parts and cost
Use generic names. Mark guesses as guesses. No invented prices or part numbers.

## Interfaces
What does it need from other tracks? What does it give to them?
