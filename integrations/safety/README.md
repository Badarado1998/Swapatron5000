# Safety

> **Draft. Nothing here is decided.** This is a starting brief. Every idea is a proposal, and every number is a guess until measured. See the [integrations overview](../README.md) and the [proposed baseline](../../DECISIONS.md).

## Goal

Spot trouble early: damp, heat, cold, tampering, damage, and anything that could hurt a person or ruin parts. Safety is the first job of every other track.

## What could go wrong

| Risk | Could be caught by |
|---|---|
| Damp or condensation in the bins | A humidity sensor, a weekly check, desiccant |
| Heat in summer, cold in winter | A temperature sensor |
| Water leaking in | A leak sensor, a roof check |
| Door left open, or forced | A door switch, a tamper switch |
| The box tipping or the post failing | A tilt sensor, a structural review, a weekly look |
| Sharp edges and pinch points, especially for kids | Design, and a person looking |
| A damaged or swollen battery left by a visitor | Rules, a sign, and a weekly look. See [swap rules](../../docs/swap-rules.md) |
| Something hot or smoking | A person. Sensors for this need expert advice |

## Principle

A sensor adds information, not safety by itself. A person who checks the box every week is the real safety system, and sensors help them. Never rely on a sensor alone for anything involving fire or injury.

## Open questions

1. What are the top five risks, in order?
2. What does the weekly check list look like, in plain words?
3. Who gets an alert, how, and what do they do?
4. What should the sign say about safety?
5. Who reviews the design before it is built?

5. Anything involving a battery, a lock, a camera, or structure needs review by a qualified person before it is built.
