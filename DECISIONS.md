# Decisions

> **Nothing is decided yet.** This page lists every open question. Each one will be decided in the open, between February 22 and March 14, 2027, after the listening phase. See the [timeline](ROADMAP.md).

## How a decision gets made

1. **Anyone raises it.** Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) or comment on an existing question.
2. **Everyone discusses it** until Sunday, February 21, 2027.
3. **Maintainers post a proposed answer** with their reasons, between February 22 and 28.
4. **Final comments** are open until March 14.
5. **The decision is written here**, with a link to the discussion.

Two things can override a community preference: the site host's approval, and safety. Both will always be explained.

New to GitHub? [How to have your say](docs/have-your-say.md) shows you how to comment and vote in two minutes.

## Proposed safety baseline

These are proposals too, but they are about safety, so they need review by the site host and by people with the right expertise, not only a vote.

- No mains power in or on the box
- No loose lithium cells in the outdoor box
- No battery charging in the outdoor box
- No collection of used or damaged batteries
- Any camera or lock needs its own review before it is built
- At events, children stay with a parent or guardian, and no adult is ever alone with someone else's child
- No soldering irons, hot tools, or loose lithium cells at the technology petting zoo

If you think one of these is wrong, say so in an issue and explain why.
