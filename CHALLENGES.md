# "This won't work because..."

Good. Tell Swapatron to its face.

Swapatron has excellent hearing, but it can only fix what it is told directly. A doubt muttered in a hallway helps nobody. A doubt written down here becomes a problem with a name, and problems with names get solved.

## The deal

1. **Every "this won't work because ___" is welcome here.** The blunter and more specific, the better.
2. **It will be received as a challenge to overcome, not a reason to scrap the project.** That is the rule for everyone, including the people who started this.
3. **You do not have to bring a solution.** Spotting the problem is a contribution, and it earns a point on the [Contributors Wall](CONTRIBUTORS.md).
4. **If you can solve one, even better.** Solved challenges earn bounty points. See [BOUNTIES.md](BOUNTIES.md).

## Challenges we already know about

We are not pretending these are solved. Each one needs someone smarter than a hungry box.

| It won't work because... | Status | First thoughts |
|---|---|---|
| Rain, humidity, and Indiana winters will ruin the electronics | Unsolved | A sealed bin with a drying pack inside a vented shell. Needs a real design |
| People will dump broken junk in it | Unsolved | Clear rules on the sign, a weekly check, "tested working" labels |
| Someone will take everything | Unsolved | Maybe that is fine. Maybe a lock on the good stuff. Open question |
| Nobody will look after it | Unsolved | A rota with real names. If the rota stays empty for a month, the box closes |
| Batteries are a fire risk | Unsolved | Proposed: no loose lithium cells and no charging in the outdoor box |
| Beginners will not know what the parts are | Unsolved | A card in every bag: what it is, and what to build first |
| Nobody new will use GitHub | Unsolved | The box and the web page come first. GitHub comes later, with a two minute guide |
| Kids under 13 can not have GitHub accounts | Unsolved | They take part with an adult. Needs a better answer |
| The lab or the property owner might say no | Unsolved | Ask early, in writing, and design around what they need |
| Volunteers lose interest after the launch | Unsolved | Small jobs, quick thanks, and a review every 90 days |
| It will cost money nobody has | Unsolved | Donated materials first. No bill should land on one person |
| An event full of kids and strangers is a safety risk | Unsolved | Parents stay, keepers work in pairs, written rules, and a review by someone who works with kids. See Decision 18 |
| The founding team will be the same three people doing everything | Unsolved | Twelve small roles, a buddy for each, two people per role, and a review at 90 days |

See one that is wrong, or missing? [Add yours](../../issues/new?template=wont-work.yml).

## How a challenge gets handled

1. It gets the `challenge` label and a thank you.
2. People pile on with ideas, experience, and "we tried that once" stories.
3. If it needs a decision, it goes into [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
4. If it needs work, it becomes a [bounty](BOUNTIES.md).
5. If it turns out to be a real reason to change the plan, the plan changes. That is the point of asking.

## The ground rules

- Go after the idea, never the person.
- "This is dumb" is not a challenge. "The door will swing into the walkway" is.
- Safety problems jump the queue. If you see one, say so loudly.
