# The dream

> **This page is a dream, not a plan.** Nothing here is promised or decided. It is here so we can all see where this could go, and so you can change it. [Add your own dream](#add-your-dream) at the bottom.

## The one sentence version

A kid walks up to Swapatron, takes a sensor, goes home, builds something, and comes back to feed it a part for the next kid.

## The first box

- It stands outside the Indiana IoT Lab, lit at night, and anyone can use it
- You can see what is inside before you open the door
- The parts are sorted, bagged, labeled, and dry
- A sign tells you in five seconds what it is and that you are welcome
- A QR code takes you to a page that shows what is in the box and what it needs
- The community looks after it, and it is never full of junk

## The box that looks after itself

- It runs on its own solar power
- It knows when it is damp inside and asks for help
- It knows when it is running low on jumper wires and adds them to the wishlist
- It tells the stewards when nobody has checked on it
- It can tell you what is inside before you make the trip

## The box as a classroom

- Your first visit is also your first step into open source
- Every part of the box was designed by someone in the community, and their name is on the wall
- Kids design modules, write the labels, and help on build day
- The box itself is a project you can learn from: the plans, the code, and the mistakes are all public
- Schools, scout troops, and clubs use it as a starting point for projects

## The box that asks and answers

- Someone posts "I need three soil moisture sensors for a school garden"
- Someone else has a drawer full of them
- The parts show up in the box that week

## Many boxes

- Any town can build one from these plans in a weekend
- Each box has its own stewards and its own character
- Boxes share designs, software, and lessons with each other, and every improvement flows back into one shared pool
- A map shows every box and what each one needs

## What we are not dreaming of

- A store. Nothing is for sale
- A dumping ground for broken electronics
- A project owned by one person or one company
- Something so clever that it stops working when the power is out

## Add your dream

This page is meant to be changed. Three ways, easiest first:

1. **Tell us.** Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) with the title "Dream: " and describe it in a sentence or two.
2. **Vote.** Find a "Dream:" issue you like and give it a thumbs up. See [how to have your say](docs/have-your-say.md).
3. **Edit this page.** Tap the pencil icon at the top right, add a line under any heading or add a new heading, then tap **Commit changes**, **Propose changes**, and **Create pull request**. [Learn GitHub in 15 minutes](docs/learn-github.md) walks you through it.

Disagree with something here? Say so the same way. Taking a line out is as welcome as adding one.

## From dream to plan

A dream becomes a plan when someone opens an issue for it, the community discusses it, and it is written into [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). Until then, it lives here.
